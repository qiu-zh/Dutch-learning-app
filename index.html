Install on iPhone

  1. Open DutchDeck in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button ⇧.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add.

After the first successful load, DutchDeck can open offline. Your words and progress remain stored on this iPhone.
