About the built-in knowledge base

This edition includes the original vocabulary plus a curated core-verb-family pack. Built-in updates merge into your existing deck without deleting favorites, intervals or review history. CEFR labels are practical learning estimates and remain editable.

Install on iPhone

  1. Open DutchDeck in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button ⇧.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add.

After the first successful load, DutchDeck can open offline. Your words and progress remain stored on this iPhone.
